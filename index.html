Step 2

Manage Agreement

Pick one agreement (from Create, from the Explorer, or by ID) and take it through its lifecycle: accept, submit, dispute, adjudicate, appeal, or read its current state.

Current state

AAccept / Cancel

Party B accepts to make the agreement binding. Party A may cancel any time before acceptance.

BSubmit Deliverable

Party A, before the submission deadline. Evidence URLs get locked into the record once a dispute is resolved.

CRaise Dispute

Party B, within the dispute window after submission. If nobody disputes in time, use the permissionless finalize_uncontested action instead.

DPermissionless Actions

Anyone can call these — they only succeed once the right condition is met. This is what guarantees no agreement can ever get permanently stuck. resolve_dispute and resolve_appeal trigger the real fetch + LLM adjudication under validator consensus, so those two can take a few minutes.

ERaise Appeal

Either party, once, within the challenge window after a verdict — but only if that verdict did not already fully favor them. Argument text only: no new evidence URLs can be introduced on appeal.

FCheck a role

Read-only — no wallet or transaction required.
